Overcoming Temporal Constraints in Directed-Energy Weapons: A PPO-Based Agentic Approach to Heterogeneous UAV Swarm Defense

23rd International Bhurban Conference on Applied Sciences & Technology (IBCAST) · 2026

Research Contribution

The study's primary contribution is the development of an Agentic AI-based Weapon-Target Assignment framework using PPO that dynamically allocates Directed-Energy Weapon resources against heterogeneous UAV swarms. Unlike fixed-priority approaches such as FIFO, the proposed method jointly considers threat characteristics, laser dwell time, and turret slew time to make adaptive targeting decisions. Through high-fidelity simulation, the study demonstrates that reinforcement learning can substantially improve neutralization performance in complex, time-constrained swarm-defense scenarios.

Causal Impact Analyzer is an interactive tool for estimating and visualizing the causal effect of interventions on time series data using Bayesian Structural Time Series (BSTS) models. ImageSense

ImageSense is an AI platform engineered to detect synthetic media and deepfakes using the reasoning power of custom-built models. Instead of passively classifying pixels, its agent actively examines media for spatial noise, anatomical anomalies, and lighting inconsistencies, generating an Explainable AI (XAI) forensic report alongside its confidence score.
